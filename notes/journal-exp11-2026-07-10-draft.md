## Headline

**The instrument's dominant axis is contamination, not position — and recompute, not
rotation, is the signature outlier.** All 12 cells ran clean (gate bit-exact, P5
sensitivity 15–50× floor in 12/12, licensing every null reading). ROT and REC are
decisively NOT equivalent at instrument precision (both sit 15–110× above the path
floor; the TOST margin is met in only 2/12 cells). Whole-signature Δ_NAIVE is
colinear with Δ_ROT in every cell (cos 0.97–0.99) and ≈orthogonal to Δ_REC in
dialogue — ROT and NAIVE share the same cache content (the evicted context's
influence baked into survivor KV; only key rotation differs) while REC's recomputed
cache has that influence removed, and THAT is the axis the signature separates. On
the pre-registered pooled metric P1 is not confirmed in Regime A alone (d_ROT <
d_REC in 6/8, one-sided p=0.125; pooled 12 cells p=0.013), but the mandated
per-family breakdown localizes the effect exactly where the pooled number cannot
see it: scale-free T3 residual-PCA distance has REC drifting 2–4× further from FULL
than ROT in 11/12 cells (supplementary paired one-sided p=0.0005). The behavior
column agrees unanimously: token-KL(FULL‖REC) > token-KL(FULL‖ROT) in **12/12**
cells. P3's dissociation appears in 3/4 doc cells (comparable token-KL, REC
signature-further). One line: eviction+re-rotation preserves the *how* better than
recompute — and what it preserves is precisely the baked-in influence of the
evicted content, the thing the exp09 diagnostics called contamination and this
project's thesis calls the continuative self.

## Pre-registered verdicts (decision rules verbatim from the amended design note)

- **P1 (d_ROT < d_REC, Regime A, Wilcoxon p<0.05): NOT CONFIRMED** on the
  registered pooled metric — one-sided p=0.125, direction favorable (6/8, median
  d_REC−d_ROT = +5.14). Pooled 12-cell p=0.0134 and the scale-free tier3 view
  (11/12, p=0.0005) both support P1's *direction* but are outside the registered
  rule (pooling and family-level tests were not pre-registered as primary).
- **P2 (doc cells ≈ floor): REFUTED** — 0/4 doc cells have both conditions <3×
  floor(b); everything is 20–110× floor. Note Δ_c is measured against FULL, so it
  includes the information loss of eviction itself (unlike exp08's mech-KL, which
  compared rot to short); "stale-block eviction is signature-free" is simply false
  at ef=0.5 — half the document is gone and the signature knows.
- **P3 (dissociation — comparable token-KL, REC signature-further): FOUND** in
  3/4 doc cells (doc-00/01/02: KL within 2×, d_REC − d_ROT > 2× floor(b));
  operationalization chosen at analysis time (spec left thresholds open). The
  dialogue cells dissociate in the *joint* pattern instead: KL_REC > KL_ROT in
  8/8 while pooled d_REC > d_ROT in only 6/8 (and cosΔΔ ≈ 0 says the two
  conditions' deviations are different in kind, not different in degree).
- **P4 (effects concentrate in T2.5+T2, not T1): NOT CONFIRMED.** On pooled Δ²
  shares everything is gate-dominated (T2+T2.5 ≈ 0.2–0.3% vs T1 ≈ 0.6%); on the
  scale-free family view the discriminating family is **T3 residual-PCA** (and
  gate stats by magnitude), NOT attention routing / cache dynamics. The phase-0
  localization intuition does not carry to this contrast: cache-condition drift
  shows up in where the residual stream sits, not in how attention routes over
  the (surgered) cache — T2.5 reads FULL-like under all three conditions.
- **P5 (d_NAIVE ≥ 10× floor in every cell): CONFIRMED 12/12** (15.0–49.6×). All
  null readings above are licensed.
- **P6 (amended: non-equivalent excess is a fade, not nonsense): PARTIAL / MIXED
  in an informative way.** Within-condition: pure fade (monotone 5/6 curves,
  directionally coherent, replication cos 1.0000). Between conditions: the ROT-REC
  excess in Regime A is different-in-KIND (cosΔΔ ≈ 0), which the amended spec's 5a
  vocabulary labels "orthogonal excess" — but the NAIVE placement (5d) resolves
  what it means: damage lies ON rotation's axis (cos .97–.99) and OFF recompute's.
  Rotation fades like forgetting; recompute moves somewhere else entirely.
- **TOST equivalence: NOT EQUIVALENT** — |d_ROT − d_REC| < 2× floor(b) in 2/12
  (need ≥10/12). The boring-null is decisively ruled out; the instrument sees
  eviction at ef=0.5 loudly under every mechanism.

## Interpretation (three sentences)

The signature instrument, pointed at the cache question, mostly measures ONE thing:
whether the shortened cache still carries the evicted context's baked-in influence.
Rotation preserves that influence (its whole-signature delta is colinear with
NAIVE's, and its residual-PCA drift from FULL is 2–4× smaller than recompute's,
11/12 cells, while token-KL favors it 12/12) — recompute deletes it, and both the
behavior metric and the character metric register the deletion as the larger
departure from the full-context self. On this axis the project's thesis comes back
confirmed in a sharper form than P1 anticipated: the mechanism error of re-rotation
is nearly invisible; what rotation "costs" is the same graceful-forgetting
direction that any eviction costs, and what recompute costs is the continuity
itself.

## Anomalies / caveats

1. **Gate-scale domination of the pooled metric.** 98–99% of Δ² lives in
   gate_features because raw pre-SiLU gate stats reach |6,178| while most features
   are O(0.1–10). The pooled pre-registered numbers are internally consistent
   (floors share the scale) but P1/P4 pooled reads are effectively gate-family
   reads; the per-family table (mandated by metric 6) is the honest lens. Future
   contrast should standardize per-feature before pooling (registered as a
   follow-up, not applied post-hoc to the primary here).
2. **NAIVE is not behaviorally catastrophic in this geometry** (KL_NAIVE ≈ KL_ROT
   ≈ 5e-3, not exp01's 2.6–4.5). The design note's premise cited exp01's
   catastrophic case, but that case DROPPED SINKS; exp11's NAIVE keeps sinks and
   preserves survivor relative order, leaving only a mid-cache positional gap —
   which Llama-3.2 at 5k positions tolerates behaviorally. P5 was defined on the
   signature axis and passed 12/12 regardless; but "NAIVE = gross cache damage"
   should be read as "gross positional surgery," not behavioral wreckage.
3. **Floor(a) = 0 exactly** (deterministic batched replay on this stack) — the
   spec's bit-stability check passed maximally; all ratios use floor(b).
4. **Exploratory 5b is uninformative here** — floor deltas project 87× isotropic
   into the standardized LDA window (scale interaction), and conditions do not
   beat the permutation null. Per the amendment: means nothing, drawn from it:
   nothing.
5. The generation-stage shape-toll saga (see above) — three iterations, resolved
   via vLLM serving from an existing conda env under Luxia's explicit
   authorization. Also 3 retok_mismatch turns (leading-whitespace strips) and
   uniformly cap-length (~340-tok, sentence-trimmed) turns in the substrate.
6. **Deviations from the spec text, all recorded:** bf16 instead of the anamnesis
   preset's fp16 (design contract wins); 3B calibration loaded from
   phase_0/outputs/calibration (spec's path holds only the 8B artifacts); the
   signature space is the 2,713-dim v3 FULL space per amendment 1 (the metric
   header's "1,837-dim" is the phase-0 figure); P3 thresholds (KL within 2×,
   excess > 2× floor) chosen at analysis time; tier3 paired test is supplementary,
   not pre-registered.

**Mass correction.** All statistics in standardized space Δz = (sig_c − sig_FULL) ⊘
FULL_scale (calibration per-feature std from `factor_directions_3b.npz`; hard
FULL_names alignment assert; 0 degenerate scale entries). Family statistic = RMS =
‖Δz_F‖/√|F| — a per-feature mean, so a 1,250-feature family cannot win by count; family
size n is reported in every table. Gap statistic = per-cell log2(RMS_REC/RMS_ROT);
per-family floors = the same RMS on that family's FLOOR_B (path-floor) delta.

**Per-family NAIVE axis: NOT uniform, and the non-uniformity is the finding.**
cos(Δz_NAIVE, Δz_ROT) is high in every family (0.84–1.00) — NAIVE and ROT are the same
deformation everywhere. cos(Δz_NAIVE, Δz_REC) spans 0.06→0.97: the families that
carry the magnitude gap are exactly the ones where REC leaves the NAIVE/ROT axis (T1

(T2_other 0.96, T2_spectral 0.97, per_head 0.83) see all three conditions as one
shared eviction direction. The headline "contamination axis" is therefore not a global
property of the signature — it is localized to residual-PCA geometry, key-content
geometry, logit statistics, and gate dynamics, and invisible to the attention-read
families. Doc cells show the same NR-high pattern with weaker gap significance (n=4;
only T3 and output reach the sign-test floor p=0.0625 there).

**One paragraph, the direct answer.** Is T3 carrying by its own weight? Yes — with the
mass question now answered in both directions. Per feature, T3's REC-vs-ROT excess is
the largest of any tier family and the only tier-level one that survives measuring
every feature against its own replay-noise floor (+1.22 log2, 12/12, p=0.0002); its
1,250-dim mass is not the source of its ranking, and the effect has structure
(mid-late layers, all snapshots, REC ≈ 2.3× ROT per feature). But T3 is not the sole
carrier: the finer vocabulary finds the same contamination signature, at equal or
larger per-feature strength, in two small families the tiers were hiding — 79 pre-RoPE
key-geometry features (+1.38 dlg, the largest per-feature gap anywhere) and 25 logit
statistics (+0.87) — and LOFO confirms the separation survives T3's removal entirely.
The instrument's contamination axis is redundantly encoded across residual geometry,
key content, and output statistics — T3 is its loudest, most robust reader, not its
container.
